For reviewing drawings, contracts, and anything else where you need to point at a spot and say "this".

## Shapes

The Shapes button in the toolbar draws five things: **Rectangle**, **Ellipse**, **Cloud**, **Line**, and **Arrow**. Click the button to draw the shape you used last, or the arrow beside it to pick another. They're in the Tools menu too.

Drag on the page to draw. The options menu has:

- **Color** for the outline.
- **Fill**, for rectangles, ellipses, and clouds. Fills are see-through, so they tint what's underneath instead of covering it.
- **Thickness** and **Opacity**.

Clouds are the revision clouds you see on construction drawings: a bumpy outline around the area that changed.

Use the same shape and color over and over? **Save as Tool…** at the bottom of the menu keeps it in the [tool chest](/guides/tool-chest) with a name, one click away.

## Moving and resizing

Switch to the Select tool ([[V]]) and click a shape.

| Do this | With this |
| --- | --- |
| Move it | Drag it |
| Resize it | Drag a corner handle. Lines and arrows have a handle at each end |
| Keep its proportions | Hold [[⇧]] while resizing |
| Nudge it one point | Arrow keys |
| Nudge it ten points | [[⇧]] with the arrow keys |
| Delete it | [[Delete]] |
| Deselect | [[Esc]] |

An unfilled shape only responds to clicks on its outline. That's on purpose: you can draw a big rectangle around a paragraph and still select the text inside it.

Change a color or thickness while a shape is selected and that shape changes. With nothing selected, you're setting the default for the next one.

## Stamps

Tools > Stamps opens the stamp picker. It has:

- **Review** stamps: Approved, Rejected, Reviewed, For Review, Draft, Confidential, and Void. Each one carries today's date.
- **Icons**: a set of general-purpose symbols.
- **Electrical symbols** for marking up plans, grouped as Power, Lighting, Switches & Controls, Low Voltage, and Fire Alarm.
- **Custom** stamps you make from your own images or words (see below).
- **Recently Used** at the top, and a search box that looks through all of them.

Pick a stamp, then click the page to place it at its normal size, or drag to place it at whatever size you want.

Stamps move and resize like shapes, and always keep their proportions.

## Your own stamps

The **Custom** section of the stamp picker holds stamps you make yourself:

- **New Image Stamp…** turns a PNG, JPEG, or SVG into a stamp: a logo, a signature block, an engineer's seal. Transparent parts stay transparent. Large images are scaled down to keep the stamp small, and SVGs become a sharp picture at that size.
- **New Text Stamp…** makes a stamp like the review stamps from your own words, such as APPROVED AS NOTED or NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION, in the color you choose. Leave **Add the date it's placed** on to print the date under the words. Letters, numbers, and everyday punctuation all work; symbols the PDF's standard font doesn't have, such as emoji or ✓, show as ? in the preview and in the saved stamp.

A new stamp is ready to place as soon as you make it. Custom stamps show up in Recently Used and in search like the built-in ones.

Click **Edit** in the Custom section to rename or delete them. Deleting a stamp only removes it from the picker; copies you've already placed stay in their documents.

Custom stamps are kept on this computer and shared by every window, up to 50 of them. They're saved into your PDFs as ordinary stamps, so other PDF apps show them, and someone who opens the file in Clumsy on another computer can still move, resize, and delete them.

## Other people's markup

Shapes, underlines, and strikeouts that someone added in another app can be selected, moved, restyled, and deleted in Clumsy just like your own. Anything attached to them that Clumsy doesn't edit, such as the author's name, stays in the file untouched.

## Shapes and stamps in the right-click menu

Both can go in the tools row of the right-click menu. Right-click the Shapes or Stamps button in the toolbar and choose **Add to Context Menu**.
