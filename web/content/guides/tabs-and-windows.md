Every document gets its own tab. Tabs live in panes, panes live in windows, and a tab can move between all of them without losing its page, zoom, or unsaved markup.

## Tabs

[[⌘T]] or [[⌘N]] opens a Start tab with your recent files. Opening a file from a Start tab replaces it, the way a browser's new-tab page works. Opening a file that's already open switches to its tab instead of making a second one.

| Do this | With this |
| --- | --- |
| Next or previous tab | [[⇧⌘]]] and [[⇧⌘[]]{{mac: , or [[⌃Tab]]}} |
| Jump to a tab by position | [[⌘1]] through [[⌘8]] |
| Close the tab | [[⌘W]] |
| Bring back the tab you just closed | [[⇧⌘T]] |

Reopen Closed Tab remembers the last 20 tabs you closed in a window, and it brings each one back on the page and zoom you left it at.

## Split panes

[[⌘\]] splits the window and opens a second copy of the current document on the right. That's the quick way to read page 12 while looking at the appendix on page 80.

You can have up to four panes. Drag a tab from one pane's tab bar to another to move it, or use the keyboard:

| Do this | With this |
| --- | --- |
| Split Right | [[⌘\]] |
| Close the split | [[⇧⌘\]] |
| Move the tab one pane over | [[⌃⌘← ~ Window > Move Tab to Left Pane]] and [[⌃⌘→ ~ Window > Move Tab to Right Pane]] |
| Focus the pane next door | [[⌥⌘←]] and [[⌥⌘→]] |

Drag the divider between panes to resize them. Closing the last tab in a pane closes the pane.

> Moving a tab between panes doesn't reload it. Your scroll position, zoom, and anything you haven't saved come along.

## Windows

[[⇧⌘N]] opens a new window. To give a tab its own window, drag it out of the window and let go, or press [[⌥⌘N]]. Drop a tab on another Clumsy window and it joins that window instead.

A file only ever lives in one window. If you open something that's already open in another window, Clumsy brings that window forward rather than opening a second copy that could save over the first.

## Go to File

[[⌥⌘P]] lists this window's open tabs, then your recent files. Type a few letters of the name and press Return. It's the fastest way to switch once you have more tabs than fit in the bar.

## Coming back later

Quit and reopen, and the Start tab lists what you had open recently. {{mac: When Clumsy restarts to install an update, it goes further: every window, pane, and tab comes back where it was, including documents with unsaved changes.}}
