Every markup in a document, in one list: shapes, stamps, measurements, highlights, drawings, text boxes, and notes, including the ones other people made in other apps. It's how a punch list gets worked through and how a takeoff turns into numbers.

## Open it

The Markups tab is in the sidebar on every PDF. Open it from the tab, with View > Markups, or with [[⇧⌘M]]. Picking a Measure tool opens it too.

Each row shows the markup's subject, its page, and, when it has them, its measurement, review status, author, and comment. Click a row to go to it. Clumsy's own markup is selected, ready to move or restyle; anything else is outlined for a moment so you can spot it.

## Counts

The list is grouped by subject, with a count beside each one, and a total length and area for groups that hold measurements:

- Duplex Receptacle (24)
- Recessed Downlight (10)
- Conduit run (3) · 186' 4"

That's a takeoff. Pick a symbol from Stamps, click once on every fixture, and the count climbs as you go. The Stamps tool stays on between clicks, so it's one click per fixture.

A stamp's subject is its name, like "Duplex Receptacle". Other markup uses its kind ("Rectangle", "Highlight") unless it has a subject of its own. Subjects that differ only in capitals or spaces count as one.

Markups made in other apps keep their subjects, so counts from another estimating tool's count markups show here too.

## Filter and sort

Type in the search box to find a subject or comment. **Filter and sort** opens the rest:

| Choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Group by** | Subject, page, or no groups |
| **Sort by** | Page, subject, type, author, date, status, or value, either way |
| **Type**, **Subject**, **Author**, **Status** | Show only that one |
| **Pages** | A range like `1-3, 5`, or **This page** |

Counts and totals always follow the filter, so "receptacles on pages 3 to 6" or "only the open items" is one filter away. The top of the tab shows how many markups are showing out of the total.

## Review status

Select a row and choose a status: **Accepted**, **Rejected**, **Cancelled**, **Completed**, or **None**. [[⇧]]-click selects a run of rows and [[⌘]]-click adds or removes one, to set a status on several at once. Setting a status can be undone.

Statuses are saved the standard way PDFs record review status, so other PDF apps show the same status, and statuses set in other apps show here.

> Highlights, drawings, and text boxes you haven't saved yet can't take a status. Save first.

## Delete

Select one or more rows and click **Delete**. It works for shapes, stamps, measurements, and notes, and can be undone.

## Copy and export

| Button | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy** | Every row showing, ready to paste into a spreadsheet |
| **Copy Summary** | One row per subject: count, total length, and total area |
| **Export CSV…** | The rows as a CSV file |
| **Export Summary…** | The summary as a CSV file |

All four use what the list is showing, filters included. The same commands are in the Tools menu.

The rows include page, type, subject, comment, author, date, status, the measurement as text and as a plain number with its unit, the scale, the color, and where it sits on the page.

## Your name

Type your name in **Your name** and Clumsy saves it as the author of markup and notes you add from then on, and of review statuses you set. It's empty until you fill it in, so your name never goes into a file without you choosing it.

Highlights, drawings, and text boxes don't record an author yet.
