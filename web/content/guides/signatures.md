This is the everyday kind of signing: your name, in ink, on the line. For signing with a certificate that others can verify, see [Digital signatures](/guides/digital-signatures).

## Make a signature

Tools > Sign{{mac:  ([[⌃⌘S]])}}, or the Sign button in the toolbar. The first time, the Add Signature sheet opens with three ways to make one:

- **Type** your name and pick a handwriting font.
- **Draw** it with a mouse, trackpad, or pen.
- **Image**: choose a photo or scan of your signature on plain paper. Clumsy lifts the ink off the paper and throws the paper away. Dark ink on a light background works best.

Whichever you choose, the signature becomes vector outlines. It stays sharp when you resize it and when the page is printed, and it doesn't have a white box behind it.

## Place it

A new signature lands on the page you're looking at. Drag it onto the line, and drag its corners to size it.

## Save it for next time

Leave **Save signature** checked in the Add Signature sheet and Clumsy keeps it. You can save up to five, which covers a full signature, initials, and a few variations.

The arrow beside the Sign button lists your saved signatures. Pick one and it lands on the page, ready to drag into place. From that menu you can also make a new one or remove one you no longer want. Tools > New Signature opens the sheet directly.

Saved signatures are kept on your computer and shared by every Clumsy window. They aren't uploaded anywhere.

## Descriptions

Each signature has a short description, such as your name. It's saved in the PDF as alt text, so a screen reader announces "Jordan Avery" rather than "image".

## Dates and initials

For a date beside your signature, use Fill In > **Today's Date** and click the page. For initials on every page, save them as a second signature.

## What this is and isn't

A signature placed this way is markup. It looks like your signature, and for most paperwork that's what's being asked for. It doesn't prove who placed it or that the document hasn't changed since. When you need that, sign [with a certificate](/guides/digital-signatures).
